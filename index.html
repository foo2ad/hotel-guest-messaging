What we do

Instant answers

Check-in and check-out times, Wi-Fi, breakfast, airport transfers and more — answered straight from your hotel's information, in your guest's language.

Staff stays in control

Complaints, payments, refunds and special requests go straight to your staff for approval. Nothing is promised without a human saying so.

Every conversation logged

Every guest message and reply is recorded, so new questions your staff answers once become part of the knowledge for next time.

How it works

  1. A guest sends a message on WhatsApp.
  2. We look up the answer in your hotel's information and reply instantly.
  3. If the question needs a person, we send your staff an urgent message with the guest's exact words — and tell the guest help is on the way.
  4. Your staff answers directly, or sends the answer back for us to relay.
